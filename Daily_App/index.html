Privacy Policy

Daily App

🔐 ADVANCED ENCRYPTION ENABLED - YOUR DATA IS PROTECTED
📱 Your Privacy Matters: Daily App is designed with **privacy and security** at its core. All your data stays **on your device** and sensitive information is protected with advanced encryption using **Android KeyStore**. We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information on external servers.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Daily App. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application. Daily App is a local, offline task management application that respects your privacy by design and protects your sensitive data with industry-leading encryption technology.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Daily App stores the following information locally on your device using **Room database** and **encrypted DataStore**:

  • **User Credentials:** Username and password (encrypted using AES/GCM encryption with Android KeyStore)
  • **Sensitive Task Data:** Passwords and other sensitive fields stored in tasks are encrypted using AES/GCM
  • **Task Information:** Your to-do lists, task titles, descriptions, completion status, and creation dates (stored locally)
  • **User Preferences:** App settings and preferences (encrypted using Android's EncryptedDataStore)
🔒 Enhanced Security: Sensitive data is encrypted using **AES/GCM/NoPadding** encryption with 256-bit keys stored securely in **Android KeyStore**. This system-level security ensures your sensitive information is protected even if someone gains physical access to your phone. **We never transmit, upload, or sync this information to any external servers or cloud services.**

2.2 Data We DO NOT Collect

Daily App does NOT collect, access, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information beyond what you create locally
  • Location data
  • Device information
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Network or browsing activity
  • Any data to third-party services

3. How We Use Your Information

The information stored locally on your device (with sensitive fields encrypted) is used solely for:

  • Authenticating your access to the app
  • Storing and managing your daily tasks
  • Maintaining your app preferences and settings
  • Providing core functionality of the task management features

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Advanced Encryption Implementation

Daily App implements robust security measures for sensitive data:

  • **AES/GCM Encryption:** Sensitive data (user credentials and sensitive task fields) is encrypted using AES algorithm in GCM mode with 256-bit keys
  • **Android KeyStore:** Encryption keys are securely generated and stored in Android's hardware-backed KeyStore system, making them virtually impossible to extract
  • **Encrypted DataStore:** User preferences are encrypted using Android's EncryptedDataStore with hardware-backed security
  • **Custom CryptoManager:** Our implementation (package: `com.oscarcruz.mydayliactivities.data.security`) ensures proper encryption/decryption of sensitive fields
  • **GCM Mode:** Uses Galois/Counter Mode for authenticated encryption, providing both confidentiality and data integrity

4.2 Local Storage Only

Daily App uses Android's **Room database** to store your data locally on your device, with sensitive fields encrypted using our custom CryptoManager. All data remains in your device's private storage, inaccessible to other applications.

4.3 No Cloud Sync

Daily App does not include cloud synchronization features. Your data never leaves your device, which means:

  • ✅ **Maximum privacy** - no one can access your data remotely
  • ✅ **Hardware-backed encryption** protects sensitive data even if device is compromised
  • ✅ No internet connection required for full functionality
  • ⚠️ **Data is not backed up automatically** - if you uninstall the app or lose your device, your data will be lost

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Lottie Animations

Daily App uses **Lottie animation files** for enhanced user interface. These are locally bundled assets and do not connect to external services or transmit any data.

5.2 No Analytics or Advertising

We do not use any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking tools. Your app usage is completely private and encrypted.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Since Daily App stores all data locally with encryption for sensitive fields and does not transmit any information, we do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to any third parties. Period.

7. Your Rights and Controls

You have complete control over your data:

  • **Access:** All your data is stored locally, with sensitive fields encrypted, and accessible only to you with your credentials
  • **Modification:** You can edit or delete any tasks or information within the app
  • **Deletion:** Uninstalling the app will **permanently delete** all locally stored data
  • **Security:** Your sensitive data remains encrypted at rest and is protected by your device's security measures

8. Children's Privacy

Daily App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. Since all data is stored locally with encryption for sensitive fields and we don't collect any information from users, the app can be used by anyone. However, parental guidance is recommended for younger users.

9. Technology Stack

For transparency, Daily App is built using:

  • **Room Database:** For local data persistence
  • **Android EncryptedDataStore:** For secure storage of user preferences
  • **Android KeyStore:** For hardware-backed secure encryption key management
  • **Custom CryptoManager:** Our encryption implementation in package `com.oscarcruz.mydayliactivities.data.security`
  • **Hilt:** For dependency injection
  • **Clean Architecture with Use Cases:** For maintainable and testable code
  • **Lottie:** For animations (local files only)

10. Encryption Standards

Daily App uses modern encryption standards:

  • **Algorithm:** AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) in GCM mode
  • **Mode:** GCM (Galois/Counter Mode) - provides authenticated encryption
  • **Key Size:** 256-bit encryption keys
  • **Padding:** None (GCM mode doesn't require padding)
  • **IV Generation:** Randomized initialization vectors for each encryption operation
  • **Key Storage:** Android KeyStore system with hardware-backed security when available
  • **DataStore Encryption:** Android EncryptedSharedPreferences/EncryptedDataStore

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and on this page. Continued use of Daily App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Permissions

Daily App requires minimal permissions:

  • **Storage:** Only to store your tasks and encrypted preferences locally on your device
  • No internet permissions required
  • No camera, location, or contacts access

4. Almacenamiento y Seguridad de Datos

4.1 Implementación de Cifrado Avanzado

Daily App implementa medidas de seguridad robustas para datos sensibles:

  • **Cifrado AES/GCM:** Los datos sensibles (credenciales de usuario y campos sensibles de tareas) están cifrados usando el algoritmo AES en modo GCM con claves de 256 bits

  • **DataStore Cifrado:** Las preferencias de usuario están cifradas usando EncryptedDataStore de Android con seguridad respaldada por *hardware*

Daily App utiliza la **base de datos Room** de Android para almacenar tus datos localmente en tu dispositivo, con campos sensibles cifrados usando nuestro CryptoManager personalizado. Todos los datos permanecen en el almacenamiento privado de tu dispositivo, inaccesibles para otras aplicaciones.

Daily App no incluye funciones de sincronización en la nube. Tus datos nunca salen de tu dispositivo, lo que significa:

  • ✅ **Máxima privacidad** - nadie puede acceder a tus datos remotamente

  • ✅ No se requiere conexión a internet para la funcionalidad completa
  • ⚠️ **Los datos no se respaldan automáticamente** - si desinstalas la app o pierdes tu dispositivo, tus datos se perderán
