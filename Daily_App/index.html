📱 Your Privacy Matters: Daily App is designed with privacy and security at its core. All your data stays on your device and is protected with military-grade encryption. We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information on external servers.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Daily App. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application. Daily App is a local, offline task management application that respects your privacy by design and protects your data with industry-leading encryption technology.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device (Encrypted)

Daily App stores the following information locally on your device using encrypted Room database and DataStore:

  • User Credentials: Username and password (encrypted using AES-256 encryption)
  • Task Data: Your to-do lists, task titles, descriptions, completion status, and creation dates (fully encrypted)
  • User Preferences: App settings and preferences (encrypted using DataStore with AES-256)
🔒 Enhanced Security: ALL data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption, the same standard used by banks and military organizations. Your data is stored ONLY on your device and is protected even if someone gains physical access to your phone. We never transmit, upload, or sync this information to any external servers or cloud services.

2.2 Data We DO NOT Collect

Daily App does NOT collect, access, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information beyond what you create locally
  • Location data
  • Device information
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Network or browsing activity
  • Any data to third-party services

3. How We Use Your Information

The encrypted information stored locally on your device is used solely for:

  • Authenticating your access to the app
  • Storing and managing your daily tasks
  • Maintaining your app preferences and settings
  • Providing core functionality of the task management features

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Military-Grade Encryption

Daily App implements multiple layers of security:

  • AES-256 Encryption: All sensitive data (credentials, tasks, preferences) is encrypted using Advanced Encryption Standard with 256-bit keys
  • Encrypted Room Database: Your task database is encrypted at rest using SQLCipher
  • Encrypted DataStore: User preferences are encrypted using Android's security best practices
  • Secure Key Storage: Encryption keys are managed securely by Android's KeyStore system

4.2 Local Storage Only

Daily App uses Android's Room database (with SQLCipher encryption) and encrypted DataStore to securely store your data on your device. All data remains in your device's private storage, encrypted and inaccessible to other applications.

4.3 No Cloud Sync

Daily App does not include cloud synchronization features. Your encrypted data never leaves your device, which means:

  • ✅ Maximum privacy - no one can access your data remotely
  • ✅ Military-grade encryption protects your data even if device is lost or stolen
  • ✅ No internet connection required for full functionality
  • ⚠️ Data is not backed up automatically - if you uninstall the app or lose your device, your data will be lost

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Lottie Animations

Daily App uses Lottie animation files for enhanced user interface. These are locally bundled assets and do not connect to external services or transmit any data.

5.2 No Analytics or Advertising

We do not use any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking tools. Your app usage is completely private and encrypted.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Since Daily App stores all data locally with encryption and does not transmit any information, we do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to any third parties. Period.

7. Your Rights and Controls

You have complete control over your encrypted data:

  • Access: All your data is stored locally, encrypted, and accessible only to you with your credentials
  • Modification: You can edit or delete any tasks or information within the app
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored encrypted data
  • Security: Your data remains encrypted at rest and is protected by your device's security measures

8. Children's Privacy

Daily App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. Since all data is stored locally with encryption and we don't collect any information from users, the app can be used by anyone. However, parental guidance is recommended for younger users.

9. Technology Stack

For transparency, Daily App is built using:

  • Room Database with SQLCipher: For encrypted local data persistence
  • Encrypted DataStore: For secure storage of user preferences with AES-256 encryption
  • Android KeyStore: For secure encryption key management
  • Hilt: For dependency injection
  • Use Cases: For clean architecture implementation
  • Lottie: For animations (local files only)

10. Encryption Standards

Daily App uses industry-standard encryption:

  • Algorithm: AES-256 (Advanced Encryption Standard)
  • Key Size: 256-bit encryption keys
  • Database Encryption: SQLCipher for Room database
  • Preference Encryption: Android EncryptedSharedPreferences/DataStore
  • Key Storage: Android KeyStore system for secure key management

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and on this page. Continued use of Daily App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Permissions

Daily App requires minimal permissions:

  • Storage: Only to store your encrypted tasks and preferences locally on your device
  • No internet permissions required
  • No camera, location, or contacts access
